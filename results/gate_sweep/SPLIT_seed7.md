## Reading rules (the point of this document)

1. **A selection number is not a result.** The EXPLORE row is where the winner was
   chosen; only the TEST row (and its CI) may be quoted as performance.
2. **Paired CIs, not point estimates.** If the TSR paired CI contains 0, the sweep has
   not earned the right to change the default, whatever the EXPLORE ranking says.
3. **Watch the FAR column.** It is the axis this knob moves in the opposite direction;
   a TSR gain bought with a FAR regression is a trade, not an improvement, and the
   objective requires FAR < 0.20.
4. **Selection on 11 sequences is noisy.** A reshuffle of `--seed` changes the halves;
   run at least two seeds before believing a ranking. If the winner flips between seeds,
   the sweep is measuring noise.
